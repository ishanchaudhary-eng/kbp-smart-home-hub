

# KBP Smart City — Real Estate Website

## Overview
A high-conversion, mobile-first real estate website for KBP Smart City township on NH-21 Kharar–Kurali Highway, Mohali. Designed to generate enquiries and site visit bookings.

## Design System
- **Colors**: Extracted from logo — Deep Navy (#1B3A5C), Rich Gold (#C5A044), with white backgrounds
- **Typography**: Modern sans-serif (Inter), large headings, generous spacing
- **Style**: Clean, minimal, premium real estate aesthetic with large imagery placeholders

## Pages & Structure

### Home Page (Single-page scrolling with section navigation)

1. **Hero Section** — Full-width with gradient overlay, project name, "NH-21 Kharar–Kurali Highway, Mohali", configurations (2BHK | 3BHK | Plots | Commercial), starting price placeholder, and a "Get Price List" lead capture form
2. **Quick Highlights** — 4 icon cards: Affordable Homes, Highway Connectivity, Gated Township, Family Friendly
3. **Project Overview** — Brief intro with key facts and stats
4. **Why Choose KBP Smart City** — Trust signals: 30+ years experience, delivered projects, secure gated township
5. **Property Options** — 3 cards linking to Apartments, Plots, Commercial sections with images and brief descriptions
6. **Amenities** — Grid of amenity cards with icons (Parks, Jogging Track, Kids Play, Clubhouse, Gym, 24/7 Security)
7. **Location Advantage** — Map placeholder with distance cards (Chandigarh, Mohali, IT Hub, Highway)
8. **Floor Plans** — Tabbed layout for 2BHK, 3BHK with image placeholders
9. **Pricing Section** — Pricing table with enquiry form embedded
10. **Investment Opportunity** — Growth stats and appreciation potential
11. **About KBP Group** — Developer credibility section
12. **Site Visit CTA** — Full-width banner with "Book Free Site Visit" form
13. **Contact / Footer** — Address, map placeholder, contact form, social links

### Additional Pages (via routing)
- **About KBP Group** — Detailed developer profile
- **Apartments** — 2BHK & 3BHK details with floor plans
- **Plots** — Residential plots info
- **Commercial** — Shop details

## Sticky Elements (Always Visible)
- **WhatsApp button** — Floating bottom-right (green)
- **Call Now button** — Floating bottom-left on mobile
- **Sticky bottom bar on mobile** — Call | WhatsApp | Book Visit
